Customer Ticket Tracking

Proposed customer-facing flows for support requests (missing order, refund, etc.) — not shipment tracking. Goal: customers can see progress without sending follow-up messages.

A. Timeline status page B. Refund staged updates C. My Requests hub D. Magic-link email updates E. Waiting-on-customer loop
